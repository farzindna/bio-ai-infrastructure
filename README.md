# AI-Integrated Genomics & Healthcare Systems Infrastructure

Welcome to the architectural core of my research and development projects. This repository serves as a centralized documentation hub and a public interface for several high-impact projects bridging **Artificial Intelligence, Clinical Genetics, and Systems Biology.**

## 🔬 Core Projects Overview

### 1. AI-Driven Genomic Analysis & Oncology
*   **Focus:** Structural insights into drug resistance (e.g., Azole resistance in *Aspergillus*) and predictive modeling for pancreatic cancer treatment response.
*   **Tech Stack:** Python, LangChain, Vector Databases (RAG), and ESMFold for protein modeling.

### 2. GenoPars: Health-Tech Charity Infrastructure
*   **Focus:** A WordPress-based ecosystem connecting charitable donors with genetic laboratory services through automated AI-generated impact reports and discount systems.
*   **Status:** Active Deployment.

### 3. InsurTech & Forensic AI Frameworks
*   **Focus:** Developing secure, rule-based AI assistants for forensic carpooling and insurance diagnostic decision support.

## 🔒 A Note on Repository Privacy & Security
Due to the **highly sensitive nature** of the data involved—including human genomic data, healthcare records, and proprietary diagnostic algorithms—the source code for the projects mentioned above is maintained in **private repositories**. 

This is to ensure:
*   Compliance with healthcare data privacy regulations.
*   Security of sensitive forensic and insurance logic.
*   Protection of ongoing academic research prior to official publication.
